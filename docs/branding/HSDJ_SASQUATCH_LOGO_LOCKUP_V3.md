# HSDJ Sasquatch Logo Lockup V3

**Status:** Concept proof for Patrick's approval  
**Working name:** Sasquatch Signal Lockup

## Purpose

Create one identifiable mark that works both as Howe Sound Wedding DJ's calling card and as a tall overlay that reduces attention on the AI dance montage without hiding its general atmosphere.

## Composition

The Sasquatch character inherits Patrick's recognizable DJ pose: headphones, lowered focus, one hand operating below the crop, and one arm raised into the room. It occupies the full vertical axis and supplies the identity at a distance.

HOWE SOUND crosses the lower torso so the character and name form one silhouette rather than two stacked assets. Both letters O are identical EQ controls with fluted edges, recessed faces, and indicators fixed at twelve o'clock.

The crossfader is the wordmark underline. Its rail travels horizontally and its tactile cap is vertical. The cap aligns with the gap between HOWE and SOUND. WEDDING DJ provides the final high-contrast read below the hardware.

Cream outlines and restrained red/blue print misregistration keep the mark readable across changing video frames and connect the polished character artwork to the site's fly-poster language.

## Overlay test

The transparent lockup has been tested against four substantially different frames from the current montage: cool outdoor light, warm outdoor light, red interior light, and dark cabin light. The character and primary wording remain visible in all four states while the footage continues to register as ambient dancing.

## Responsive rule

Desktop and large mobile treatments may use the complete lockup. At smaller sizes, the SQUAMISH, BC micro-line may be removed first. The fader ticks may simplify next, but the two knob O forms, vertical fader cap, Sasquatch pose, HOWE SOUND, and WEDDING DJ must remain.
