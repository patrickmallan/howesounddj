## 1. Executive verdict

The repository contains enough strong human and wedding evidence to begin planning the CH 00 source strategy, but not enough verified, proprietary material to begin CH 00 composition exploration responsibly.

The strongest available sources show:

- Patrick performing
- Patrick as a warm, approachable person
- Real-looking wedding emotion and full dance floors
- Sea-to-Sky mountain context in some images
- One particularly useful Sunwolf/DJ-equipment scene
- A short, energetic dance-floor video
- A deep bank of customer language and local wedding knowledge

The central weakness is that the repository documents the **results** of Patrick's work far better than the **physical working life** that should generate the new identity.

There are currently no repository assets showing, in a verified and reusable way:

- Patrick's actual controller or mixer in close detail
- Faders, cue buttons, pads, labels, LEDs, wear, or serial plates
- Flight cases, gaffer tape, stickers, cables, speaker hardware, or setup markings
- Handwritten set lists, notes, request sheets, or planning annotations
- Serato cue markers, crates, or waveform material approved for public use
- Load-in, sound-check, teardown, weather, road, or behind-the-scenes moments
- Old HSDJ flyers, business cards, posters, or Wix-era visual fragments
- A vector or editable master of the existing logo
- Recorded image provenance and usage permission

That means the repository can currently support a more photographic redesign, but it cannot yet support the proprietary physical interface language described by the constitution without inventing authenticity.

### Readiness assessment

- **Governance readiness:** Complete and frozen
- **CH 00 content clarity:** Strong
- **CH 00 photographic potential:** Moderate to strong, pending provenance confirmation
- **Physical-ancestry source readiness:** Weak
- **Full identity readiness:** Not ready
- **Production implementation readiness:** Intentionally not authorized

### Gate recommendation

Do not begin CH 00 static composition studies until Patrick completes the P0 provenance decisions and supplies at least the minimum physical-artifact kit in Section 8.

## 8. Minimum artifact kit to unlock CH 00

CH 00 does not require a twenty-year archive. The minimum high-value kit is:

1. Provenance approval for two existing Patrick/wedding images
2. One top-down photograph of Patrick's actual controller or mixer
3. Three close equipment details
4. One flight-case, tape, or label photograph
5. One privacy-safe handwritten note or set-list fragment
6. One Squamish working-context photograph
7. One old HSDJ item with genuine character, if available

This can be captured with a modern phone. Consistency is less important than truth.

### Capture guidance

- Use the highest available resolution.
- Avoid portrait mode and artificial background blur.
- Photograph both straight-on and at one natural working angle.
- Include some surrounding context.
- Do not over-clean equipment.
- Avoid bright reflections hiding labels.
- Preserve the original files; do not send only social-media screenshots.
- Photograph private/client material separately so it can be redacted before use.
- Include one ruler-free wide shot and multiple tight details rather than trying to make every image a finished hero.

## 11. Final conclusion

The repository already contains the emotional outcome: weddings, crowds, Patrick, and flashes of Squamish. It does not yet contain enough of the machinery, handwriting, wear, logistics, or historical residue that would make “The Website Is the Deck” uniquely true rather than merely a strong art-direction idea.

The next move is not more doctrine and not production code.

It is a small, deliberate act of collection:

1. Confirm what the existing images really are.
2. Capture the minimum artifact kit.
3. Build the governed source-library manifest.
4. Then authorize CH 00 composition exploration.

The redesign will become difficult to imitate only when its source material becomes difficult to imitate.
