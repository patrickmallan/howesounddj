# Howe Sound DJ — Sasquatch video overlay V4

## Purpose

This is a video-specific treatment of the Howe Sound DJ identity. It is deliberately larger and more translucent than the primary logo so the brand becomes the foreground image while the dance-floor montage supplies movement and atmosphere behind it.

## Locked composition

- Use the 16:9 lockup across most of the video frame without extending beyond the video container.
- The Patrick-pose Sasquatch is centred, enlarged, and runs from the top to the bottom of the frame.
- Two faceted mountain walls frame a dominant central summit. The centre is both higher and broader than the outer peaks, preserving the original mountain-range hierarchy behind the Sasquatch.
- `HOWE SOUND` uses the approved Geometric Floor direction.
- Every letter uses one clean typographic layer with no overlap or registration-shadow duplicates. Spacing is measured from the visible rendered outlines—not the typeface's nominal text boxes.
- Each EQ knob replaces one O exactly: the knob has the same visual width as an O and uses the same compact 14–15px letter gap as the surrounding glyphs. `HOWE` and `SOUND` retain a distinct 77px word space.
- The knobs use the supplied Pioneer-style control as material reference while remaining top-down: a fluted black-metal perimeter, machined face, dimensional dark centre and a reflected starburst distributed sideways and diagonally across the face. The white position indicator is isolated from the reflection and locked at 12 o'clock on both knobs. Avoid playful button-like colour clusters.
- The actual fingerprint light from the original dark playback clip is isolated as a separate permanent brand disc behind and to the left of the first O knob. It renders independently at full opacity beneath the translucent main lockup. Its opaque dark backing plate masks both changing footage and the original occurrence, so the same dense concentric motif remains identical throughout every clip.
- `WEDDING DJ` uses the same Geometric Floor face as the main wordmark. It is freestanding with no card, underline or swoop. Yellow is not used in this lockup.
- Do not use a crossfader in the overlay. At video scale it reads as an unrelated object and competes with the character.

## Video treatment

- Desktop overlay opacity: 60%.
- Small-screen overlay opacity: 66% to retain legibility at reduced size.
- The montage is silent, ambient motion. Do not frame it as proof or invite the visitor to press play.
- The logo must obscure enough facial and motion detail to soften AI artefacts while still allowing colour, light and dancing to move through it.
- Keep the cream face and dark keyline crisp. Do not use red/blue offset copies on this wordmark; its geometry and spacing must remain professional at video scale.

## Separation from the primary logo

Do not apply the 60% transparency rule to the primary identity everywhere. The translucency is a deliberate response to moving footage. Static uses should retain enough opacity and contrast to function as a conventional mark.
