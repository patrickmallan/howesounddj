# HSDJ Logo Exploration V1

**Status:** Research and direction selection  
**Primary name:** Howe Sound Wedding DJ  
**Compact mark:** HSDJ

## Brief

Build a recognizable identity from the physical and cultural language of DJing without relying on Squamish mountain silhouettes or generic music symbols. The mark should feel authored, tactile, direct, and credible in both a wedding setting and a late-night dance-floor setting.

Avoid mountains, headphones, music notes, generic vinyl records, equalizer clichés, glossy app-logo effects, and stock badge construction.

## Route 01 — Signal Route

A compact monogram constructed from channel rails, a fader cap, and a routed signal cable. The route connects preparation and control with the movement of a live room. It is the most explicitly equipment-derived option.

## Route 02 — Channel Splice

The name becomes the equipment: a crossfader rail cuts through the wordmark and a blue fader cap creates the identifying gesture. It is direct, scalable, and capable of becoming a repeatable visual behaviour across navigation, motion, video, print, and signage.

This is the recommended primary direction for refinement. It has the clearest silhouette and the best balance of wedding credibility, DJ-world specificity, and long-term flexibility.

## Route 03 — Load-In Stamp

A stacked fly-poster and flight-case mark with a compact HSDJ stamp. It feels physical, local, and event-built. It is strongest as a secondary identity system for posters, cases, apparel, section stamps, and social material rather than as the sole primary mark.

## Recommended development

Refine Channel Splice as the primary logo and borrow the rough, applied character of Load-In Stamp for supporting brand applications. The current typography is provisional; the selected route should be custom drawn and delivered as clean outlined vectors.

The final system must work in one colour, at favicon size, over moving video, on a performance pad, on a flight case, in embroidery, and on venue-facing documents. Motion should be restrained and meaningful: the fader may travel once to reveal or energize the mark, rather than looping as decoration.
