# HSDJ Sasquatch Logo Direction V2

**Status:** Concept for Patrick's review  
**Direction:** Sasquatch at the Controls

## Correction from V1

The previous Channel Splice drawing did not depict a mechanically credible crossfader and functioned more like a banner than a complete logo system. V2 corrects both issues.

A crossfader travels horizontally, while its tactile cap is a vertically oriented grip. In this direction the horizontal crossfader sits below HOWE SOUND and the vertical cap aligns with the space between the words.

## Core identity

The Squamish Sasquatch is the memorable brand character. The mountain is removed. The Sasquatch operates a recognizable DJ mixer with one hand on an EQ control and the other carrying the loose dancing gesture from Patrick's supplied animation.

The identity has three related forms:

1. A tall primary lockup designed to cover and soften the AI dance video.
2. A standalone Sasquatch DJ emblem for pads, social avatars, signage, stickers, and motion.
3. A horizontal compact lockup for headers and narrow applications.

## Equipment details

Both letters O in HOWE SOUND become identical EQ knobs. The recognizers are a fluted perimeter, recessed circular face, controlled top highlight, and a clear indicator line. Both indicators remain at the same neutral twelve-o'clock position unless deliberate motion gives a specific reason to change them.

The crossfader includes an A/B travel rail, scale ticks, a centre position, and a vertically oriented tactile cap. Decorative controls that do not contribute to recognition are excluded from the reduced mark.

## Next refinement

The Sasquatch emblem study is an AI-assisted drawing reference, not finished logo artwork. After direction approval it must be manually simplified into outlined vector geometry, especially the hands, fur edge, mixer controls, and small-size silhouette. Typography and spacing also remain provisional until the emblem proportions are locked.

The supplied clean Sasquatch dance clip should be explored later as a short, intentional brand signature—such as a small interstitial or loading flourish—not as another large background video.
