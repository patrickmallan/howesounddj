================================================================================
COLLAGE DEPTH RULE
================================================================================

At MEDIUM and PEAK intensity, important compositions should contain at least
three genuine depth relationships such as:

A covers B.
B disappears behind C.
C breaks through A.
Typography crosses an image edge.
Hardware emerges through torn paper.
A photograph overlaps into the next physical plane.
A machine surface is revealed through a rip.
Tape physically bridges two materials.

Do not satisfy this numerically.

Use it as an art-direction test.

The viewer should perceive layers sitting over and under one another.

Not merely elements positioned beside one another.

================================================================================

================================================================================

Do not rely primarily on clean CSS polygon clipping.

looks "torn."
