## 8. Chapter composition briefs

### CH00 — Arrival

**Emotional job:** Immediate recognition, authored impact, and anticipation.  
**Intensity:** 70 / MEDIUM moving toward PEAK.  
**Must communicate:** Howe Sound DJ, wedding DJ, Squamish, Patrick, real wedding energy, check date, consultation.

Explore a total-viewport composition rather than retaining expressive-left/empty-right zoning. Machine, poster, and photograph should alter one another structurally. The strongest hero image of Patrick with his hand in the air remains an authorized source candidate, alongside approved public HSDJ photography.

The opening must not spend the page's maximum volume.

**Headline:** `PROVISIONAL / REWRITE REQUIRED`

### CH01 — Sound Check

**Emotional job:** Prepared calm and full-event competence.  
**Intensity:** 40 / LOW.  
**Must communicate:** Patrick handles more than the dance floor; preparation, ceremony, reception, communication, and technical readiness matter.

Potential structure:

- a stripped-back real mixer/channel-strip ancestor;
- four real knobs, switches, or tape labels as uneven information anchors;
- white gaffer tape, technical notes, or setup evidence;
- one human working image or equipment detail; and
- intentional breathing room without luxury-site emptiness.

Do not use a feature grid, four equal cards, or icons beside service claims.

### CH02 — Build

**Emotional job:** Show that the night is shaped over time.  
**Intensity:** 60 / MEDIUM.  
**Must communicate:** ceremony → cocktails → dinner → speeches/first dance → open floor, with different musical and operational judgment at each stage.

Potential structure:

- faders as a temporal architecture;
- a traverse across a mixer channel or rail;
- uneven stage lengths reflecting the actual emotional sequence;
- photographs and labels entering as the night opens up; and
- calm control becoming movement.

Do not create a standard timeline, numbered process row, or five equal stages.

### CH03 — Read the Room

**Emotional job:** Make musical intelligence legible.  
**Intensity:** 70 / MEDIUM-HIGH.  
**Must communicate:** the couple's taste is the source; Patrick mixes live, reads guests, handles requests, and can move toward a club-night ending when that is the brief.

Potential structure:

- EQ, filter, gain, cue, or waveform vocabulary with defensible meaning;
- real photographs interrupting or covering the controls;
- controls responding to the reading path rather than behaving as fake toys;
- faster cuts and increased visual movement; and
- contrast between planned source material and live room response.

Do not suggest drum & bass, house, disco, and throwbacks are indiscriminately mixed all night.

### CH04 — Peak

**Emotional job:** Make the outcome physically felt.  
**Intensity:** 100 / PEAK.  
**Must communicate:** real crowd release, real wedding context, live energy, and credible hard proof.

Potential structure:

- the wildest approved crowd photograph or documentary sequence;
- torn flyer and photographic planes overtaking the machine;
- huge, interrupted type;
- stereo meters driving toward yellow/red;
- a real waveform if it adds temporal meaning;
- current rating/review proof; and
- short customer language punched through the composition.

This chapter should exceed CH00. It must remain readable enough that the evidence is not reduced to texture.

Do not place a review grid beneath a dramatic photograph.

### CH05 — Operator

**Emotional job:** Let the couple meet Patrick after the peak.  
**Intensity:** 30 / LOW.  
**Must communicate:** Patrick's character, local roots, working principles, calm judgment, and genuine presence.

Potential structure:

- warm off-white paper or another physical quiet surface;
- a real portrait or behind-the-scenes photograph;
- handwriting or one real personal artifact;
- one oversized knob, cable, label, or equipment detail; and
- first-person copy with irregular but calm rhythm.

Do not create a portrait card beside an agency biography.

### CH06 — Room Remembers

**Emotional job:** Deepen trust through what real people and real nights left behind.  
**Intensity:** 50 / LOW-MEDIUM.  
**Must communicate:** planning care, packed-floor results, team feeling, real attribution, and selected wedding/venue context.

Potential structure:

- reviews as fly-poster fragments, record sleeves, taped evidence, marked-up contact sheets, or printed remnants;
- two or three substantial readable reviews rather than many excerpts;
- story and venue images with traceable captions; and
- the feeling of artifacts accumulated across nights.

Do not use testimonial cards, floating quote panels, or a conventional carousel.

### CH07 — Encore

**Emotional job:** Convert at renewed energy and finish the set.  
**Intensity:** 80 / HIGH.  
**Must communicate:** check the date, what happens next, the consultation alternative, contact/navigation, and no-pressure clarity.

Potential structure:

- the machine returning with maximum functional clarity;
- one dominant physical CUE availability control;
- a final poster-plane composition;
- consultation as a clearly subordinate physical control;
- practical next-step copy; and
- footer information organized like a real equipment specification plate.

Do not use a centered rounded CTA well or detach the footer from the visual system.

## 11. Interaction, accessibility, and performance contract

Even as research, the prototype must establish a believable usable system.

### Interaction

- Navigation pads, CUE, PLAY/consultation, and any control-like object must behave or be clearly inert.
- Route names should live on the physical pad faces when visual inspection supports it.
- Optical alignment is required; mathematical centering alone is not acceptance.
- The page must retain a plain-language next action.

### Accessibility

- Preserve semantic heading and source order.
- Preserve real link/button names under physical treatments.
- Demonstrate visible keyboard focus.
- Demonstrate a complete reduced-motion form.
- Ensure decorative material is excluded from the accessibility tree.
- Keep essential copy legible at narrow width and 200% zoom.
- Do not use colour alone for state.

### Performance

- No autoplay media.
- Avoid WebGL, heavy 3D, and unnecessary libraries.
- Prefer CSS and small purposeful scripts.
- Record image-format, font, and motion risks for production.
- Use research assets freely enough to judge composition, but document every item that requires optimization or replacement.

## 12. Anti-repetition audit

Before presentation, compare all eight chapters side by side and answer:

- Which chapters share the same underlying skeleton?
- Where did image-left/text-right or text-left/image-right return?
- Where did equal widths, cards, grids, or centered CTA composition return?
- Is every chapter using the same torn corner, colour ratio, type scale, or hardware prop?
- Did quiet chapters become generic editorial website sections?
- Did loud chapters become interchangeable club posters?
- Does each chapter have a distinct reading path?
- Could any chapter move elsewhere on the page without changing its meaning?
- Is CH04 actually louder than CH00?
- Does CH05 provide enough release for CH07 to rise again?

Any chapter that is merely CH00 at another volume must be recomposed.

## 15. Rejection conditions

Reject or rework the prototype when:

- it is an expressive CH00 followed by familiar website sections;
- the same composition skeleton repeats across chapters;
- originality depends mainly on yellow, cobalt, red, grain, monospace, or torn edges;
- hardware appears without business meaning or believable physical ancestry;
- photographs return to polite rounded containers;
- reviews become cards with poster styling;
- planning becomes a feature grid or standard timeline;
- Patrick becomes a generic agency founder or invented club persona;
- Squamish is reduced to a scenic background;
- the site is maximum volume throughout;
- CH00 is louder than CH04;
- CH05 fails to create intimacy and release;
- CH07 returns to a generic CTA block;
- mobile becomes stacked desktop;
- controls are optically misaligned or falsely interactive;
- wedding, service, place, or next action becomes unclear;
- accessibility or performance is deferred as production cleanup;
- unverified copy or evidence is presented as fact; or
- the explanation is more distinctive than the page.
