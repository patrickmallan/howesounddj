## Interpretation and decision

**Targeted raw-capture follow-up, 2026-10-14 afternoon PDT:** A third observation of P1 was made in a different browser session on the **same local date** as this second pass. Completed full answer text, direct source links and test conditions are preserved separately for [Google Search](PHASE_1_RAW_P1_GOOGLE_20261014.md) and [ChatGPT Web Search](PHASE_1_RAW_P1_CHATGPT_20261014.md). Both completed pricing answers omitted HSDJ from their answer text. A fresh, unpersonalized ChatGPT [R1 service-fit capture](PHASE_1_RAW_R1_CHATGPT_20261014.md) on the same afternoon recommended HSDJ first with an official homepage link, illustrating the intent contrast. These are durable, targeted examples, not retroactive raw evidence for the original 12-question pass or a third independent-date baseline. The files contain generated-answer claims for audit, **not** endorsed prices, review facts or provider rankings.

The direction from October 13 held: **all four ChatGPT price-intent answers omitted HSDJ as a provider**, while all five completed non-branded ChatGPT recommendation/service-fit answers listed HSDJ (often first). Google was more variable: this day's Squamish and live-mixing Overviews included HSDJ, Whistler budget and Pemberton cost did not, and the Whistler destination overview added HSDJ on a completed repeat. This sample is too small and context-dependent for a visibility percentage or a causal “price page fixes AI” claim.

The site has two specific answer-quality risks: a Google Overview implied HSDJ local booking eliminates travel/hotel fees, and ChatGPT inferred an HSDJ budget from competitor rates. The proposed guide's private-quote language, explicit source attribution, and individual travel caveat are the right *editorial* response; whether they change AI answers remains unproven. The Whistler redirect continues to produce a first-party citation that lands on Squamish; Patrick chose to keep it, so record and monitor rather than silently reverse it.

**Decision:** Continue with the narrow cost-driver guide and FAQ correction as a **local review draft**, without disclosing HSDJ rates. Both competitor-owned package pages were rechecked live on 2026-10-14 and still displayed the drafted amounts and scopes; see the [price ledger](PHASE_3_PUBLIC_PRICE_LEDGER.md). The draft's visible “checked” date is updated. **Do not publish** before Patrick approves exact copy and release. At publication, recheck the offers again. The later targeted full-text P1/R1 captures provide a reproducible starting point for post-change observations, but this 12-question pass remains summary-only and cannot support precise pre/post percentages.
