## Commercial interpretation and limits

The most actionable gap is **research usefulness at pricing intent**, not proof that HSDJ is invisible to AI search. The site already has strong service-fit retrieval. Search Console shows a separate local-page crawl lag, but the representative venue page is live-indexable and that lag is not established as the pricing-answer cause. Publishing a price-safe explanation could help couples compare quotes and help source systems extract a first-party answer; citation and revenue effects remain hypotheses until measured. The existing availability journey had 26 raw checks, 22 complete events, three scheduled consults and no booked-outcome attribution at Phase 0; these are not AI referrals or revenue.

**Approval gate:** No production deploy, live pricing-guide publication, crawler-policy change, service-area redirect reversal, or confidential-rate disclosure is authorized by this research package.
