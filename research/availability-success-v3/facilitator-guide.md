## Script

**Intro:** "I'm going to show you a quick screen from a wedding DJ website. Imagine you're planning a wedding and checking if a date is open. There are no wrong answers."

**Task:** "Check whether June 15, 2028 is available and continue as you naturally would."

**Do not explain:** Stephen, Patrick, Sound Check, or which button to press.

**Post-task questions (verbatim):**

1. What just happened?
2. Is the wedding date available?
3. Who is Stephen?
4. Why do you think his quote is shown?
5. What impression do you have of Patrick?
6. What do you expect when you tap Meet Patrick?
7. Is the meeting free?
8. How long is it?
9. Anything confusing?
10. Anything excessive or sales-like?
11. What do you remember most?
12. What would you tell your partner?
13. Would you press the button? Why or why not?
14. Did you notice how to try another date?
