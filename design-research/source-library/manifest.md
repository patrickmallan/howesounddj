## Approval states

- `PENDING`: Awaiting source, authenticity, permission, or privacy information
- `QUARANTINED`: May be inspected but may not be used in composition studies
- `RESEARCH_ONLY`: May inform analysis but may not appear in public-facing work
- `COMPOSITION_APPROVED`: May appear in a named pre-code composition mission
- `PRODUCTION_APPROVED`: May be transformed and moved into a governed production path for a named use
- `REJECTED`: Must not be used

`COMPOSITION_APPROVED` does not imply `PRODUCTION_APPROVED`.
