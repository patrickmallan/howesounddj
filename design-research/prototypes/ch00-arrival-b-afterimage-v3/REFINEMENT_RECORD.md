## Response to Patrick's critique

V2 was a loud poster with a conventional CTA and a protected headline box. V3 keeps the colour aggression, severe photography, cropped scale, hard geometry, and incompatibility, then changes the underlying behavior:

- The headline is printed onto the yellow chassis, passes under Patrick, breaks through a red event strip, and returns over the image. There is no headline container.
- The top rail is not decorative metadata. It expresses the real span of Patrick's service: ceremony, dinner, dance floor, last song.
- Check Your Date is a single illuminated physical control with a visible resting face, depressed depth, status lamp, and explicit outcome.
- Book a Sound Check becomes the secondary square control on the same physical housing rather than a second button floating elsewhere.
- Study-only labels are separated from the proposed interface language. `PRIMARY ACTION` and invented channel metadata are removed.
- `YOUR PEOPLE. YOUR WEDDING.` is removed. The core promise returns to the stronger singular statement: `A packed dance floor that still feels like your wedding.`
