## Mission and visitor question

The section should answer, “What does Patrick actually do that makes the technical side dependable and the party feel live?” It should give one memorable operational detail and one human proof point without asking a couple to read four symmetrical mini-essays.

## Species A — speech-check editorial spread

**Files:** `species-a-desktop.svg`, `species-a-mobile.svg`.  
**Physical ancestor:** A sound-check sequence: before a microphone opens, conditions are checked.  
**Transferred behavior:** A visible progression through coordinator, camera crew, mic signal, and speaker readiness.  
**Not transferred:** Fake meters, fake signal readings, decorative knobs, imagined production sheets.  
**Reading path:** First five seconds: “Before a speech, I check four things” → four real checks. Deeper scan: equipment crop → credential line → Cassandra's named proof.  
**Intentional contradiction:** Dramatic typographic scale for very plain operational details.  
**Closest familiar web pattern:** A “how I work” feature section. It avoids equal tiles and skill badges; the risk is becoming an oversized checklist.  
**Anti-Design Auditor:** ADVANCE WITH CONDITION. Keep the list terse and source-backed; remove the mixer photo if it reads as filler.  
**Conversion Guardian:** ADVANCE WITH CONDITION. The section earns trust; it does not need its own CTA. Mobile must preserve all four checks and attribution without hiding behind the image.  
**Sacrifice:** Formal training receives less space than actual wedding-day practice. That is intentional because the latter is more useful to a couple.

## Species B — couple quote as the main event

**Files:** `species-b-desktop.svg`, `species-b-mobile.svg`.  
**Physical ancestor:** A record sleeve or editorial spread in which one piece of listener response gets the largest type.  
**Transferred behavior:** Lead with real audience evidence, then turn the sleeve over to see the work behind it.  
**Not transferred:** Fake track numbers, fabricated review context, faux distressed paper, unlabeled client imagery.  
**Reading path:** First five seconds: Cassandra's exact published excerpt and name. Deeper scan: four pre-speech checks and compact credential note.  
**Intentional contradiction:** A bright, spacious quote on a normally dark site; technical facts follow in a darker band.  
**Closest familiar web pattern:** Testimonial banner. The risk is a giant marketing quote with background art. The escape is a short exact quote and a specific operational sequence immediately after it.  
**Anti-Design Auditor:** REWORK unless Patrick likes the stronger visual interruption; it may be too poster-like for a credibility section.  
**Conversion Guardian:** ADVANCE WITH CONDITION. Quote attribution and first concrete service detail remain visible on mobile; no extra CTA.  
**Sacrifice:** The credential is no longer the first proof. That can be right for a couple choosing a DJ, but formal technical qualification becomes a deeper-read detail.
