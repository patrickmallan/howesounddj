# Art-Direction Audit

- Does the work still read as the approved CH00–CH07 composition? **Yes.** Chapter sequence and 70→40→60→70→100→30→50→80 energy curve are unchanged.
- Does it feel built from photographic, printed and machine planes? **Yes.** CH00, CH03, CH04, CH06 and CH07 each contain multiple real occlusions and depth relationships.
- Are photographs material rather than tilted postcards? **Yes.** Documentary images are cropped into torn, overprinted planes that collide with typography and equipment.
- Is CH04 louder than CH00? **Yes.** It adds two photographic planes, torn fluorescent sheets, rating proof and the sole enlarged living VU.
- Is CH05 a genuine release? **Yes.** One Patrick portrait, one restrained machine detail and a quiet paper field replace the peak collision.
- Is the colour physical rather than digitally flat? **Yes.** Fibre, fold, toner, brushed-panel grain, uneven ink density, halation and localized LED spill contaminate the five-colour system.
- Are generic cards avoided? **Yes.** Reviews and proof behave as pasted/torn evidence remnants.
- Is there one meter language? **Yes.** Every prominent meter is generated by the living HSDJ VU system; the CH04 photographed meter is removed.
- Are navigation and CTA controls mechanically legible? **Yes.** Pads, CUE and PLAY are rebuilt controls, optically centred, keyboard focusable and certified at 200%.
- Does any `source-library/09 - knobs` screenshot appear in output? **No.** Those files remain crop references only.
