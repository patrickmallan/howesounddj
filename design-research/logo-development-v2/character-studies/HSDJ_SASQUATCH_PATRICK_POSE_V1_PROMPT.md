# HSDJ Sasquatch — Patrick Pose V1

## Generation method

Built-in image generation, using `patrick-dj-action.png` as the strict pose and composition reference. A chroma-key correction pass was used because the generator's first transparency attempt returned a painted checkerboard rather than an alpha channel.

## Final character prompt

Transform the entire human DJ in the reference photograph into a genuine full Sasquatch character while preserving the exact pose, body proportions, crop, energy, and gesture. Do not put Patrick's human head on a Sasquatch body. Replace the human head, face, hair, arms, hands, torso, and visible skin completely with one coherent, mature Pacific Northwest Sasquatch.

Keep the head angled downward beneath black over-ear DJ headphones. Preserve the fully extended raised arm and loose two-finger dance-floor hand sign. Preserve the lower arm angle and shape the hand as though it is resting on and operating DJ equipment immediately outside the bottom crop; do not add the equipment itself.

Use realistic anatomy with a bold editorial collage silhouette: dark charcoal-black fur, restrained cream highlights, subtle electric-blue rim light on the left and signal-red rim light on the right. Avoid a human face, face-swap appearance, ape or gorilla caricature, cute cartoon styling, sports-mascot styling, aggression, costume seams, malformed hands, equipment, scenery, mountains, text, badges, borders, and watermarks.

## Output

The production PNG has a genuine alpha channel and has been checked over both cream and near-black backgrounds.
