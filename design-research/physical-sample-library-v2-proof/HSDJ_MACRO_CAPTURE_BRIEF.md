# HSDJ Hardware Macro Capture Brief

The V2 proof can validate extraction quality, but a proprietary production library should be photographed from Patrick's actual equipment.

## Camera setup

- Use the highest-resolution rear camera; capture RAW or HEIF Max when available.
- Do not use portrait mode, digital zoom, filters, or artificial blur.
- Mount the camera directly above the equipment. The sensor plane must be parallel to the mixer/controller faceplate.
- Use a bubble level or camera grid to remove pitch, yaw, and roll before capture.
- Do not use angled photographs, three-quarter views, side views, or perspective correction for composable controls.
- Clean only loose dust. Keep honest wear, fingerprints, tape residue, and scratches.
- Photograph in broad, even overhead light plus one low-light powered-on pass, without moving the camera.
- Brace the phone or use a tripod. Tap the exact control to lock focus.
- Leave at least 20% clear space around the object so its full shadow and bezel survive masking.

## Required frames

- Full mixer/controller: one high-resolution, edge-to-edge, directly overhead master with all four corners visible.
- EQ knob, trim/gain knob, filter knob, browse encoder: directly overhead only.
- CUE and PLAY/PAUSE: powered off and powered on, framed individually.
- Six illuminated pad colours: one pad per frame where possible; never photograph a screen capture of a pad bank.
- Channel-fader cap: directly overhead, plus the full rail from the same camera axis.
- Tempo-fader cap and rail.
- One switch, one connector/jack, one screw, one worn panel edge, one cable end.

## Minimum delivery gate

- Original files only—no screenshots, messaging-app compression, or social-media downloads.
- The isolated object must occupy at least 1000 pixels on its long edge in the original frame.
- Focus must be visibly sharp at 100% zoom.
- No object edge may leave the frame.
- Reflections may describe the material but must not erase labels or contours.
- Round controls must remain circular and rectangular controls must retain parallel edges in the unedited source. Any visible keystone distortion fails the frame.

This capture set is the cleanest route to hardware artwork that cannot belong to another DJ.
