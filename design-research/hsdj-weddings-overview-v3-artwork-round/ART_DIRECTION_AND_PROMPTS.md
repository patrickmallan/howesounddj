# Weddings Overview — Human Joy Artwork Round

## Locked reference

`public/images/hsdj-redesign/weddings-overview/speeches-signal-art-v1.png`

Status: **approved as the visual benchmark**.

Why it works: it reads as an observed room before it reads as a collage. The people, candlelight, microphone, and dinner remain believable. The torn paper, cyan trace, monochrome fragments, and red marks add tension without replacing the human moment.

## New governing rule

**Documentary truth first. Art direction second.**

The crowd and its relationships carry the emotion. Equipment is incidental or absent. Treatments remain mostly at the perimeter and must never be used to manufacture joy, repair an unconvincing face, or turn the scene into spectacle.

Avoid:

- fantasy sunsets and over-processed HDR;
- plastic skin, duplicated people, or identical smiles;
- visual effects laid over faces;
- coloured smoke, confetti, festival lasers, and rainbow neon;
- packed-floor clichés;
- glossy wedding-ad perfection;
- waveform overlays used as decoration;
- hard photo-album borders.

## Ceremony candidate prompt

Use the approved speeches artwork as a style reference only. Create a credible documentary moment immediately after the vows: the couple mostly in profile or from behind, guests reacting to one another, a hand finding another hand, damp eyes, laughter, and wind moving fabric. Use natural mountain light, imperfect framing, true skin tones, deep forest and charcoal. Add only restrained editorial treatment at the perimeter: subtle grain, one or two torn monochrome fragments, a fine cyan line, and a pin of red. No DJ equipment, fantasy lighting, stock posing, perfect symmetry, or effects over faces.

## Dinner candidate prompt

Use the approved speeches artwork as a style reference only. Catch a real table at the instant one guest makes the others break into laughter: somebody leaning back, somebody covering their mouth, a hand still holding a fork, glasses and half-finished plates left imperfectly in place. Shoot at table height with warm candlelight and believable cool room spill. Restrict the collage treatment to grain, one torn monochrome fragment, slight print misregistration, and a thin cyan trace near the glassware. No DJ, posed toast, banquet-brochure polish, fake bokeh, or identical smiles.

Candidate:

`candidates/dinner-joy-candidate-v2.png`

## Celebration candidate prompt

Use the approved speeches artwork as a style reference only. Show eight to twelve friends and family later in the night, centred on one believable relationship: a guest laughing while another wraps an arm around them. Include imperfect gestures, a loosened tie, moving fabric, and cropped bodies entering the edge of the frame. Use natural low-light motion, warm faces, restrained cyan side light, one torn black-and-white contact-print fragment, and one red registration mark at the edge. No mixer, festival effects, huge crowd, confetti, glamour retouching, or frozen jumping pose.
