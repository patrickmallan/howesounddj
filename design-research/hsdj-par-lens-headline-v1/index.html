Howe Sound Wedding DJ / PAR lens headline study V1

Letters built from light.

The square message-board cell is gone. These letters use circular fixtures with visible housings, retained off-state rings and broad lighting cues that move like a programmed rig.

● Three cues running / one shared renderer

Cue A / Rotating colour wash

The fixture moves. The letters stay put.

A wide colour bank travels around the centre of the headline. Every lit cell keeps a visible lens and housing, so the word never collapses when the colour passes.

Art direction / first call

Advance the banks. Borrow the lens from all three.

Strongest identityAlternating banks feel most like an actual lighting cue. They are organized, physical and noticeably different from an LED sign.

Best for difficult artworkThe musical pulse creates the least competition when the background already carries colour and movement.

Use carefullyThe rotating wash has energy, but it belongs on only the largest H1. Smaller headings should keep the circular lens static.
