One integrated opening field was built at 1440 x 1000 and translated intentionally for 390 x 844. It establishes Howe Sound Wedding DJ, Squamish, Patrick, a wedding crowd, the headline, the date-check action and the consultation action without explaining the interface concept.

## Intentional contradiction

The warm, crowded wedding photograph collides with a cold, exacting controller surface. Patrick is visibly joyful and human while the machine remains industrial and controlled. The contradiction is managed through one clear headline and one dominant action rather than softened into a luxury-wedding layout.

## Training-set gravity audit

- Closest familiar web pattern: text-left / image-right hero
- Evidence of that pattern: the headline begins left of Patrick's photograph
- Regression risk: high if the rail, continuous controller substrate, photo crop or cross-boundary type are normalized during later implementation
- Escape: the page is one faceplate, the headline crosses the machine/photo seam, the photograph is not a contained panel, navigation is a real pad bank, the VU owns an edge and the fader owns the division
- Conventional element retained: literal navigation and CTA language, because the visitor must understand the service and next action immediately
- Verdict: `ADVANCE FOR PATRICK REVIEW`

## Anti-Design Auditor

- No cards, pills, glass panels, generic icons or symmetric feature grid survived.
- A generated waveform screen was present in the first render. It duplicated the real hardware display without carrying information and was removed.
- The system is at risk of regressing into a split hero if the overlapping headline, environmental controller field or fader seam is cleaned up.
- The faint `MIXED LIVE` overprint is the least essential surviving element and may be removed if Patrick reads it as styling rather than voice.
- Verdict: `ADVANCE`, with the split-hero regression warning preserved.
