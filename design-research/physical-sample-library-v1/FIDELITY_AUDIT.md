## Rejection checks

- No CSS-drawn control is present.
- No SVG or generic control redraw is present.
- No generative fill or image regeneration is present.
- No final sample uses a low-resolution screenshot when a higher-resolution official source exists.
- No sample is enlarged beyond native resolution in exported certification sheets.
- The fader cap reads as a wide, low, physical cap rather than a tall website slider.
