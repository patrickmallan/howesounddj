- Navigation and CTAs remain semantic links with plain-language names.
- The headline remains actual text in logical source order.
- Decorative collage is outside the accessibility tree.
- Focus, touch targets and reduced-motion behavior pass.
- No canvas, WebGL, framework, autoplay media or added continuous animation was introduced.
- A production build should convert large opaque research imagery to optimized AVIF/WebP, retain alpha only where necessary, and replace unlicensed hardware photography before launch.
