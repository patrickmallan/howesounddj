# Contact artwork — mixer as wedding floor V5

**Status:** research only; no production mutation.  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Patrick direction:** A photographed mixer cannot be treated as sufficient artwork. The equipment needs an artistic premise and should become part of the wedding story.

## Artistic premise

The mixer becomes the venue.

Miniature wedding guests occupy the channels as a working dance floor. Illuminated meters become event lighting, channel strips become pathways, knobs become sculptural objects in the room, and two DJ hands make the equipment visibly active. The HSDJ mountain and torn-paper language passes through the mixer instead of framing a product photograph.

The resulting artwork is then used as the entire Contact field. The date question occupies the quieter landscape side while the date controls cross into the active mixer/dance-floor side. The check-date action therefore moves toward the party rather than sitting in a separate form card.
