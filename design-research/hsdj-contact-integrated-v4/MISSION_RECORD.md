# Contact page — integrated artwork refinement V4

**Status:** research revision; production remains frozen.  
**Date:** 2026-09-19  
**Patrick correction:** Artwork must never sit apart from written content. The whole composition is the artwork; imagery, type, controls, and response must be intertwined and relational.

## What V3 got wrong

V3 placed the HSDJ collage in an isolated crop on the upper-right side of the page and left the date interaction on a separate black field. That produced a conventional copy/form-versus-image split, even though the edge was irregular. It does not match the visual logic established on the home page and it fails Patrick's requirement that artwork and content operate as one thing.

## V4 correction

- The wedding-night collage runs beneath the entire working field rather than occupying an image zone.
- A real DJ mixer surface enters the same collage at the lower edge; it is not a separate equipment card.
- The headline crosses both image and darkened texture so the image cannot be read as decoration beside the copy.
- The date fields are inserted into the composition's signal line. Their underlines continue into the action, making the interaction part of the artwork.
- The available result replaces the date controls in the same visual field. It does not introduce a new card or detached testimonial container.
- The review is set into a quieter portion of the artwork after the consult action, using only a rule and typographic change for separation.
- Mobile re-crops the same relational field; it does not stack an image block above a form block.
