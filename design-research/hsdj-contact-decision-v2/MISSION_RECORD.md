## Physical ancestry and anti-template check

The date stage borrows the legibility of a DJ source-selection area: choose the input, see its state, then perform the next action. It does **not** draw fake knobs or pretend a wedding date is a sound channel. The available state changes emphasis from checking to a single bright action, as a real control changes state when engaged. The visual language is spare because the business decision is more important than an art effect here. Actual DJ equipment artwork can frame the final page only if it never competes with fields, result, or scheduling action.

Closest conventional ancestor: a lead form and success card. The deliberate departure is that Contact is a two-state decision sequence rather than a contact hero, multiple CTA cards, repeated reassurance, and a long form. Proof follows the action instead of delaying it. This still needs Patrick's taste check, responsive implementation design, and accessible interaction testing.
