## Rotary correction

The original V3 rotary pass used small crops from the complete XDJ-AZ master and failed visual inspection. The active rotary family now comes from larger official DJM-A9 top-down detail renders. `browse-encoder.png` was removed because the photographed side wall made the object read as an angled view.

The EQ and Sound Color FX knobs use tight scalloped alpha contours around the removable hardware itself. The white horseshoe markings surrounding those controls in the official source are fixed mixer-faceplate graphics and are deliberately excluded. Natural reflections on the photographed knob faces remain intact.

## Rendering rule

These assets are intentionally small physical controls cut from full-device masters. They may be displayed at native size or smaller. They must not be enlarged into poster-scale hardware. Whole-device imagery or a new dedicated overhead macro source is required for that use case.
