## Anti-repetition audit

- No two adjacent chapters share the same dominant surface.
- CH00 uses a split fader seam; CH01 uses one contained paper object; CH02 uses horizontal time; CH03 uses a vertical EQ invasion; CH04 uses total-field impact; CH05 uses portrait silence; CH06 uses accumulated evidence; CH07 returns to the machine.
- Yellow/blue/red ratios change materially rather than repeating one poster recipe.
- Physical sources change by chapter: pads, channel strip, faders/waveform, EQ/rotary, meters, cable, evidence wall, controls/spec plate.
- Quiet chapters retain physical ancestry and do not revert to generic editorial cards.
- CH04 is the largest type, densest collision and most active meter state.
- CH05 is the lowest density and only predominantly off-white human chapter.
- CH07 regains colour and control without copying CH00.

**Anti-repetition verdict:** ADVANCE FOR PATRICK'S VISUAL-SYSTEM JUDGMENT.

## Anti-Design Auditor verdict

The prototype has escaped the principal failure mode of an expressive opening followed by generic sections. The full-page identity depends on structural shifts, real supplied equipment imagery, real human images and changing density—not only on colour and distress.

Remaining risks:

- Impact remains partly dependent on unlicensed equipment references and system display type.
- CH00's provisional headline is visually dominant and may distort judgment of the final opening voice.
- CH02 uses a consciously provocative provisional headline and should not be mistaken for approved copy.
- Production could regress if these compositions are normalized into reusable two-column sections.

**Verdict:** ADVANCE AS ART-DIRECTION RESEARCH. NOT PRODUCTION-ACCREDITED.

## Accessibility and performance forecast

- Semantic headings follow CH00–CH07 order.
- Navigation pads are links with explicit accessible names and visible real-text labels.
- CUE and PLAY remain semantic links with plain-language accessible names.
- All primary physical targets meet or exceed 44px in desktop and mobile audits.
- Keyboard focus is visible and captured.
- Decorative VU and machine material carries no required meaning.
- Reduced-motion mode removes smooth scrolling and transitions while preserving the complete page.
- No audio, video, canvas, WebGL, framework or new dependency is used.
- JavaScript is limited to VU illumination, the CH00 fader and active chapter navigation.
- Production would require responsive optimization of the large documentary photographs and replacement/clearance of research hardware.

**Forecast:** Suitable for research evaluation; production accessibility, loading, source rights and real availability states still require chapter accreditation.
