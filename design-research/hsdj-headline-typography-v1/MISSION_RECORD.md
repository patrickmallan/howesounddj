## Mission

Design a headline voice that belongs to Howe Sound Wedding DJ instead of substituting another fashionable web font. It must hold long, plain-language headlines; connect visibly to HSDJ's physical and cultural sources; remain readable on mobile; and avoid becoming a novelty “DJ font.”

## Emotional job

The type should make the writing feel louder, stranger, and more authored before texture or photography is added. It should carry the impact of a techno bill and punk paste-up while retaining the control of professional DJ equipment.

## Required content

- The current Weddings headline as a long-form stress test.
- A shorter dinner headline as a medium-form stress test.
- The HSDJ footer line as a modular display stress test.
- Uppercase letters and numbers represented before production selection.

## Physical ancestry record

### Crossfader Grotesk

- **Physical ancestor:** crossfader rail and cap; photocopied event bill; misregistered print.
- **HSDJ source artifact:** `public/images/hsdj-redesign/controls/faders/crossfader-rail.png`, `crossfader-cap.png`, and the approved DJM surface.
- **Business meaning:** primary page headlines.
- **Transferred behavior:** a controlled horizontal interruption crosses an otherwise forceful continuous word.
- **Transferred visual property:** condensed mass, rail cut, slight registration drift.
- **Not transferred:** fake scales, fake data, or a control that appears interactive.
- **Why HSDJ:** it joins the site's actual fader language to its gig-poster typography without requiring a hardware picture behind every headline.

### Channel Stencil

- **Physical ancestor:** channel strip separators, engraved faceplate labeling, overprinted handbill.
- **HSDJ source artifact:** approved DJM top-down surface and channel-line system.
- **Business meaning:** short section headlines and emphatic editorial interruptions.
- **Transferred behavior:** two consistent channel breaks divide the letter bodies.
- **Transferred visual property:** black equipment engraving colliding with paper, cobalt, and red ink.
- **Not transferred:** military stencil conventions and arbitrary serial numbers.
- **Why HSDJ:** the breaks come from the physical channel system already used across the site.

### Meter Matrix

- **Physical ancestor:** DJM-A9 master VU meter.
- **HSDJ source artifact:** `public/images/hsdj-redesign/controls/meters/djm-a9-master-meter.png`.
- **Business meaning:** rare peak words, numbers, short labels, and visual punctuation.
- **Transferred behavior:** characters illuminate as discrete level segments.
- **Transferred visual property:** segmented construction and green/yellow/red escalation.
- **Not transferred:** continuous animation, fake audio response, or arcade scoring.
- **Why HSDJ:** the alphabet is constructed from the exact meter language used by the site rather than applying a generic digital font.

## Intentional contradiction

Warm wedding language is carried by cold machinery. The type is forceful while the writing stays plain and human.

## Training-set gravity audit

### Crossfader Grotesk

- **Closest familiar pattern:** oversized condensed agency/editorial display type with chromatic registration.
- **Evidence:** heavy all-caps, tight leading, cyan/red offset.
- **Regression risk:** the print effect could become a fashionable glitch treatment pasted onto a standard font.
- **Escape:** redraw the glyphs; build the interruption into each character; allow irregular alternate forms; remove chromatic registration at lower scales.
- **Conventional remainder:** a condensed grotesk skeleton because the current language is long and must remain readable.
- **Verdict:** ADVANCE TO GLYPH DRAWING.

### Channel Stencil

- **Closest familiar pattern:** brutalist poster typography.
- **Evidence:** cream field, black type, oversized cuts, abrupt primaries.
- **Regression risk:** could become generic punk styling or military stencil.
- **Escape:** derive every cut height from the HSDJ channel system and draw non-military terminals and counters.
- **Conventional remainder:** uppercase display hierarchy for fast scanning.
- **Verdict:** ADVANCE AS A SECONDARY/ALTERNATE SOURCE.

### Meter Matrix

- **Closest familiar pattern:** LED sports board, arcade, or gaming identity.
- **Evidence:** luminous pixel construction and RGB level escalation.
- **Regression risk:** strong at any scale; easily overwhelms weddings and Patrick.
- **Escape:** restrict to short labels, numerals, and one-word peak moments; never paragraphs, actions, or long H1 copy.
- **Conventional remainder:** 5 × 7 construction for reliable legibility.
- **Verdict:** HOLD AS A RARE ACCENT.

## Anti-Design Auditor verdict

Crossfader Grotesk is the only candidate broad enough to become the primary headline voice, but the current specimen is still a treated familiar grotesk. It is not approved as a font until its actual glyph outlines are redrawn and the hardware interruption changes intelligently with the letter anatomy. Channel Stencil has value as an alternate character source. Meter Matrix is authentic but too literal to lead the brand.

## Conversion Guardian verdict

Crossfader Grotesk remains legible across the tested long headline and preserves plain-language comprehension. Channel Stencil should be limited to medium and short lines. Meter Matrix is unsuitable for instructions, navigation, calls to action, or essential long-form headings. Body text remains Geist; interface labels remain Geist Mono or a later governed equipment-label face.

## Mobile relationship

The same species remain on mobile. Crossfader Grotesk reduces in width and registration distance rather than stacking every word individually. Channel Stencil reduces the cut thickness and moves its rail. Meter Matrix reduces segment size but does not change into a conventional font.

## Sacrifices

- A custom headline system costs more design and QA effort than importing a font.
- Crossfader Grotesk will not be neutral; it must therefore be reserved for display hierarchy.
- Meter Matrix cannot carry long copy even though it has the strongest literal DJ ancestry.
- Genuine custom glyph drawing must follow approval; the prototype effects are not final font outlines.

## Patrick decision request

Decide whether the primary direction should be:

1. **Crossfader Grotesk** — controlled, condensed, loud, and most usable;
2. **Channel Stencil** — rougher, stranger, and more poster-led; or
3. a hybrid using Crossfader construction with selected Channel cuts and alternate letters.

Decision received: Meter Matrix will be drawn as the real display font. Its production colour system will use bone-white for reliable reading plus controlled magenta, ultraviolet, cyan, and occasional warm signal accents. The site will not apply a full rainbow to every headline.
