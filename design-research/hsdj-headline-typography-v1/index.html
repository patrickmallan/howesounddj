Howe Sound Wedding DJ / Type laboratory V1

Not a DJ font.An HSDJ font.

Three headline species built from fader rails, channel interruptions, meter segments, fly-posted type and the pressure of a room with the system turned up. These are research specimens, not production fonts.

Species A / Crossfader Grotesk

Condensed, physical, interrupted

Designed to carry the biggest HSDJ headlines without turning every word into a novelty effect.

Species C / Meter Matrix

A real modular alphabet

Built from individual meter segments. Strong HSDJ ancestry, but intentionally tested as a limited accent rather than the default headline voice.

Ancestor / DJM master meter

This is the only specimen whose letters are genuinely constructed rather than treated. Its risk is obvious: too much becomes an arcade, festival, or gaming identity instead of a wedding DJ with taste.

The first call: build from A, steal carefully from B, keep C rare.

Designer

Advance Crossfader Grotesk. It has enough hardware ancestry to belong here without forcing every headline to perform a trick. Channel Stencil can supply alternates and shorter section hits. Meter Matrix belongs on numbers, one-word peaks, and equipment labels.

Anti-Design Auditor

Species A still begins with a familiar condensed grotesk. It only advances if the final glyphs are actually redrawn, the cuts vary intelligently by character, and the registration effect is optional—not the identity.

Conversion Guardian

All three read at headline scale. A is safest for longer language. B needs careful mobile line breaks. C fails for paragraph-length headlines and must never carry essential instructions or buttons.
